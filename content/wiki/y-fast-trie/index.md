title: y-Fast Trie
---

A y-Fast trie splits nodes into blocks. Each block is a balanced search tree and has 2/w to 2w nodes. The block maximums constitutes an [♯ x-Fast Trie]({{< relref path="/wiki/x-fast-trie.md" lang="en" >}}).
